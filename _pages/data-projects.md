        <h3 class="data-project__title">Reading the election-night count</h3>
        <p class="data-project__meta">Report · CA / ES · October 2026</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <p class="data-project__desc">On election night, polling stations are not counted in random order: small towns report first and big cities last, so provisional results can mislead for hours. This report shows how a simple weighting of the stations already counted, based on the previous election, anticipates the final result well before the count ends.</p>

        <h3 class="data-project__title">Language ABM Explorer</h3>
        <p class="data-project__meta">Interactive app · Shiny · EN</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <p class="data-project__desc">An agent-based model of language choice between Catalan and Spanish speakers in Catalonia. Speakers are loyal, adaptive or rigid, and each conversation is a coordination game. Change the share of Catalan speakers, homophily or the mix of speaker types to see how the share of conversations held in Catalan responds, compared with a baseline calibrated on 2023 survey data.</p>
